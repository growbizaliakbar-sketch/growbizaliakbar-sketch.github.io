🎀 Gudiya Sajao

Gudiya ko apni pasand se sajao — kapray, baal, rang, jootay aur pyari pyari cheezein.

Gudiya Tayyar Hai! 🎉
👗 Kapray (Dress)
🎨 Kapron Ka Rang
💇 Baal (Hair Style)
🎨 Baalon Ka Rang
👟 Jootay (Shoes)
✨ Pyari Cheezein (lagao / hatao)
🏠 Jagah (Background)
Kaise khelein: Neeche diye button dabao aur gudiya badal jayegi. Kapray chuno, un ka rang chuno, baal banao, jootay pehnao, taaj aur bag lagao, aur jagah bhi badlo. Har tabdeeli par Style Points milte hain. Jab gudiya bilkul saj jaye to "Gudiya Tayyar" wala button dabao. "Surprise" dabane se gudiya khud-ba-khud naye rang roop mein aa jayegi.
