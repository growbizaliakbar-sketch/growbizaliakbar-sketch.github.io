Turbo Drive

Traffic se bacho, qareeb se guzro ge to extra points.
Morr par gaari bahar ki taraf jati hai, sambhalna!
90 seconds hain — jitna tez, utna score.

Keyboard: ← → chalana, ↑ tez, ↓ brake, Space = Nitro
Mobile: screen ke left/right hisse ko dabao, gaari khud tez chalti hai, NITRO button dabao

Kaise khelein: Aap ki neeli gaari neeche hai. Upar se aati gaariyon ko overtake karo — bilkul qareeb se guzarne par "Near Miss" ke extra 50 points milte hain. Takkar honay par speed gir jati hai aur waqt zaya hota hai. Har 1000 meter par 5 second extra milte hain. Road ke kinare lage darakht aur board dekh kar speed ka andaza hota hai.
