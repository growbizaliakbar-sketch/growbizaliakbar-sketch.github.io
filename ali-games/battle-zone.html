Battle Zone

9 dushman bots ke saath maidan mein utro.
Neela zone chhota hota jayega — zone se bahar rahe to health giregi.
Dawai (✚) uthao, sab ko khatam karo, winner bano!

Keyboard: WASD / Arrow keys se chalo, Mouse se nishana, Click gedabao = fire
Mobile: left side ungli ghumao = chalna, right side dabao = fire (nishana khud lagta hai)

Kaise khelein: Aap hare rang ke khiladi ho. Baaki 9 bots aik doosre se bhi larhte hain. Goli lagne se health kam hoti hai; zameen par pari dawai uthane se health barhti hai. Zone ka ghaira har kuch second mein chhota hota hai — safed dairay ke andar raho warna health tezi se giregi. Sab se aakhir tak zinda rahe to jeet tumhari.
