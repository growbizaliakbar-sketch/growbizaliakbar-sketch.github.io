Air Strike

Aap ka jahaz khud fire karta hai — bas chalana hai.
Taakat (W) uthao ge to goliyan dugni ho jayengi.
Har wave mein dushman zyada, aur aakhir mein BOSS aayega!

Keyboard: Arrow keys / WASD se jahaz chalao  |  Mobile: ungli se jahaz ko drag karo

Kaise khelein: Jahaz khud-ba-khud goli chalata hai, aap ko sirf usay chalana aur dushman ki goliyon se bachana hai. Chhota dushman jahaz = 50 points, drone = 30, Boss = 500. W wali taakat lene se 12 second ke liye dugni goliyan chalti hain. 3 lives hain — takkar ya goli lagne se ek life jati hai.
