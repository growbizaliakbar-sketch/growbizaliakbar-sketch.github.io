Speed Dodge

Start dabao, gaari ko left-right kar ke
dushman gaariyon se bachna hai.

Keyboard: ← → ya A / D  |  Mobile: ungli drag karo

Kaise khelein: Aap ki green gaari neeche hai. Upar se aati laal gaariyon se takrana nahi hai. Har kuch seconds mein speed barhti hai. Score aap ke survive time par banta hai, aur best score aap ke phone/browser mein save rehta hai.
