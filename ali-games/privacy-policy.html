Privacy Policy

Hum kaunsi maloomat lete hain?

Ali Games par khelne ke liye koi account ya personal maloomat nahi chahiye. Hum aap ka naam, email ya phone number collect nahi karte. Aap ke game scores sirf aap ke apne browser/device mein save hote hain (local storage) — wo hamare paas nahi aate.

Cookies aur advertising

Ye website mustaqbil mein Google AdSense ke zariye advertisements dikha sakti hai. Google aur us ke partners cookies istemal kar ke aap ki pehle ki visits ki bunyaad par ads dikha sakte hain. Aap apne browser settings se cookies band kar sakte hain, aur Google ki ad settings se personalized ads off kar sakte hain. Ads aane par ye policy update kar di jaye gi.

Third-party links

Is website par mojood bahari links (masalan WhatsApp) ki privacy policies un ki apni hain, hum un ke zimmedar nahi.

Bachay

Ye games aam khail hain aur har umar ke liye mehfooz hain. Hum jaan boojh kar bachon se koi personal maloomat collect nahi karte.

Tabdeeli aur rabta

Is policy mein koi tabdeeli ho to isi page par update kar di jaye gi. Sawal ho to Contact page se rabta karein.
